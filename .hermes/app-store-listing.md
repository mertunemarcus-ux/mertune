## Description (4000 chars max)
Mertune is the live map of everything happening in Florida.

Open the map, zoom into Florida, and every stadium, arena, theater, concert hall, cruise terminal and seasonal attraction lights up as a dot. Tap one and the Event Posters panel slides in: what's happening TODAY, what's NEXT UP, and everything coming — with the real event poster, date, time, and a direct link to tickets.

NO MORE SEARCHING TEN WEBSITES
Kaseya Center, Hard Rock Stadium, Amerant Bank Arena, LoanDepot Park, Nu Stadium, Fillmore Miami Beach, Broward Center, Arsht Center, Raymond James, Camping World Stadium, EverBank Stadium, Daytona International Speedway and dozens more — one map, one tap.

ALWAYS CURRENT
Mertune refreshes itself every 4 hours, around the clock. Last-minute games, surprise shows, price drops — if it's on sale, it's on the map.

SEE IT, THEN GO
• 🔴 TODAY badge — what's on right now, with the poster
• ⏭ NEXT UP — the next show at every venue
• Directions in one tap
• Get Tickets straight from the panel

MORE THAN CONCERTS
• NBA, NHL, NFL, MLB, MLS and college football
• Broadway tours, ballet, comedy, opera
• Cruises departing PortMiami — every terminal, every line
• Seasonal: Halloween haunts, holiday shows (red dots, gone when the season ends)

SEARCH LIKE GOOGLE
Type an artist, a team, a venue or a street — Mertune finds it instantly.

CURRENTLY IN BETA
Florida is live now. More states are unlocking soon. Found a bug or a missing venue? Tell us — this app is built with its users.

Mertune. Know what's happening in the city.
