Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026
The short version: Mertune does not require an account, does not collect your name, email, contacts, or precise location, and does not sell data. The app shows public event information on a map and links you to ticket sellers.

1. Who we are

Mertune ("we", "us") is a live-events map for Florida, available as an iOS app, an Android app, and at mertune.com. Contact: mertuneco@gmail.com.

2. Information we do not collect

  • No account or sign-in is required. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or password.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or calendar.
  • We do not collect precise GPS location. The map is navigated by tapping, not by tracking you.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA/AAID) and we do not run third-party ad networks inside the app.

3. Information processed automatically

When the app loads map content from our servers and from map-tile providers, standard technical data such as your IP address, device type, operating system version, and request timestamps may be processed by those servers for the purpose of delivering the content and keeping the service secure. This data is not used to build a profile of you.

  • Hosting: Vercel, Inc. (app content) — privacy policy
  • Map tiles: CARTO / OpenStreetMap contributors — privacy policy
  • Event data & images: Ticketmaster Discovery API and venue websites — publicly published event information only

4. Links to ticket sellers

Tapping "Get Tickets", "Book Cruise", or an event poster opens the seller's website (for example Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, a venue's box office, or a cruise line) in your browser. Those sites are operated by third parties with their own privacy policies; any purchase, account, or payment information you provide there is collected by them, not by Mertune. Some links may be affiliate links, meaning Mertune may earn a commission from the seller at no extra cost to you. Affiliate tracking is performed by the seller's platform, not inside the Mertune app.

5. Children

Mertune is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Data retention and security

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf. Server logs held by our hosting providers are kept according to their own retention policies. All app traffic uses HTTPS.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Since Mertune holds none, there is nothing to action, but you are welcome to contact us at mertuneco@gmail.com with any question.

8. Changes to this policy

If Mertune adds features that change how data is handled (for example optional accounts or saved favorites), we will update this page and the effective date above before those features ship.

9. Contact
